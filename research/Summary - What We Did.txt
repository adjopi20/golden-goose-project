Based on the last Golden Goose research conversations, the useful summary is this:

Overall answer

Yes, the work was useful, but not because we already found a tradable edge.

It was useful because we eliminated weak ideas, clarified what has signal, and found that most simple order-flow variables are not strong enough alone.

The main conclusion:

Nth bubble / confirmed setup rank has some structure.
Post-rank bubble activity has some information.
But the pattern is not clean enough yet to become a trading rule.
What we tested and found
1. MFE + efficiency confirmation

We tested whether a bubble that immediately creates favorable movement and efficient movement has better future result.

Finding:

Yes, MFE and efficiency are useful as setup confirmation filters.

This helped define a “confirmed setup” instead of treating every large bubble as meaningful.

Useful? Yes.
This became the foundation of the confirmed setup generator.

2. Nth confirmed setup / Nth bubble rank

We tested whether the Nth confirmed bubble inside an impulse group relates to max_R_before_SL.

Finding:

Rank 1 is useful as impulse anchor, but not always the best entry.
Rank 3–5 looked more stable.
Rank 9 sometimes looked interesting, but sample was smaller and less reliable.

The relationship was not linear.

Meaning:

More bubbles does not simply mean better result.
The useful zone is delayed, but not endlessly delayed.

Useful? Yes, but not final.
It gave us a direction: do not blindly enter first impulse confirmation.

3. Master export architecture

We changed the architecture so impulse_bubble_master.parquet becomes the main source of truth.

Finding:

This was useful technically, not statistically.

It made later research easier because we can reconstruct:

confirmed setups
rank positions
same-side/opposite-side bubbles
impulse groups

Useful? Yes.
This improves research workflow and avoids fragmented outputs.

4. Post-rank-3 900s evidence window

We tested:

After rank 3 appears, wait 900 seconds.
Observe same-side/opposite-side bubble activity.
Then enter after 900s and calculate max_R_before_SL.

Finding:

Blind rank3 + 900s entry was not strong.
Overall median was below 1R.

But the fight-state filter had some separation:

neither_same_nor_opposite was best.
both_same_and_opposite was weak.
opposite contra-move was dangerous.
qty imbalance was useless.

Most interesting sub-finding:

short + no qualified bubble activity after rank 3 looked strongest.

Useful? Partially.
It showed that post-rank evidence contains information, but not clean enough.

5. Post-rank-3 300s evidence window

We tested the same logic with 300 seconds instead of 900 seconds.

Finding:

300s was also weak as a blind rule.
The best states were only slightly better than others.
No clean separation.

Interpretation:

300s may preserve more opportunity for longs.
900s looked better for short no-fight continuation.
But neither was strong enough overall.

Useful? Mostly as elimination.
It told us not to keep parameter-hunting this branch too much.

6. Same-side vs opposite-side bubble behavior

Across several tests, we examined whether same-side or opposite-side bubble behavior explains result.

Finding:

Raw same-side count/qty is not enough.
Raw opposite-side count/qty is not enough.
Qty imbalance correlation was basically zero.

But reaction type mattered more:

opposite contra-move = warning
opposite absorbed = not necessarily bad
mixed same+opposite fight = usually noisy / weak

Useful? Yes conceptually, weak statistically.
It tells us that “bubble side” alone is too crude. Reaction quality matters more than raw bubble count.

Final summary of usefulness
Useful findings
1. MFE + efficiency confirmation is useful.
2. Rank / Nth confirmed setup has some structure.
3. Rank 3–5 is more interesting than rank 1 as final entry.
4. Opposite-side contra-move is a consistent warning.
5. Mixed two-sided fight usually weakens the setup.
6. Quantity imbalance is not useful.
7. Master export architecture is useful for future research.
Not useful enough
1. Blind rank3 entry is not enough.
2. Blind rank3 + 300s delay is not enough.
3. Blind rank3 + 900s delay is not enough.
4. Same-side-only after rank 3 is not a strong standalone signal.
5. Bubble count and bubble qty are not strong predictors.
6. The post-rank-3 fight-state idea is not strong enough to continue deeply right now.
Practical conclusion

This research branch produced a clear answer:

There is structure, but not enough edge yet.

So the work was not wasted. It helped narrow the search.

The current strongest research direction is not:

Find the best Nth bubble.

It is more likely:

Use confirmed setup rank as context, then search for a better market-state or invalidation condition.

In other words:

Nth bubble tells us where we are in the impulse.
It does not by itself tell us whether the trade is worth taking.

So yes, moving to the next research is the correct decision.
